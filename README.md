## 🌟 Selected work

### 🥄 SpoonFull: AI dietary monitoring
**Django · Angular · Tauri · MySQL · Computer vision**
Scans each meal tray (photo + weight) to measure what residents ate, then gives guideline-based diet feedback.
- **In daily use** at a long-term care facility in Chiayi, Taiwan
- Intake records went from **~15 min to ~10 s** each; about **$23K/yr** in net operating savings
- 🏆 2nd Place, International Category, 21st DSP Creative Design Contest (Taiwan MOE) · 📄 Paper accepted to ICBET 2026

### 🧱 Concreat Rewards: partner rewards portal
**React · TypeScript · Vite · Vitest**
Proposed rewards-program rules turned into a clickable portal that covers every partner role, program and reward flow.
- **Proposal approved** by Concreat Holdings Philippines
- In simulation, each 5-point retention gain adds about **10% to 5-year sales**

### 📇 HubSpot CRM cleanup & reporting
**HubSpot workflows · Python · Dashboards**
Cleanup, address syncing, lead-source tagging and a daily dashboard for a US diagnostics company's CRM.
- Leads needing manual review cut from **324 to 22**
- **395 duplicates** caught before import

### 📬 Lead follow-up reminder
**Make.com · Google Sheets · Gmail**
Reads a lender lead tracker and sends the finance manager one weekly email of follow-ups, owners and next actions.
- About **93% less** weekly review time

### 📦 Paagi: packaging & transport recommender
**Angular · Django · Roboflow**
Recognizes an item and recommends the packaging and transport that fit it best.
- 🏆 **Champion, SCS Days 2025 Hackathon**; in simulation, **72% less** wasted box space
